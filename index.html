GOOD TO KNOW

Questions, answered.

A simple process, with expectations made clear.

How do I request an errand?

Join the WhatsApp group or message the team directly. Describe the task, location, deadline and instructions. Wait for confirmation before assuming a request is accepted.

How much does an errand cost?

Ask for a quote before work starts. The total may depend on distance, waiting time, urgency, transport and items purchased. Confirm the full breakdown first.

Are item costs separate from the service fee?

For purchase errands, agree on the item budget and service fee separately in chat. Keep receipts where available and verify the recipient before sending money.

Can every task be accepted?

No. Requests depend on availability, safety and campus rules. CatchUp does not complete assignments on a student's behalf, facilitate cheating or handle prohibited items.
